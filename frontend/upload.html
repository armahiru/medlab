Clinical uploader

Seal Medical Report

Select a registered patient, attach the clinical file, and seal it onto the medical chain. The patient is notified automatically.

What happens when you seal

1) File → SHA-256 hash 2) Metadata + hash → new block 3) Block linked to previousHash 4) Patient alert created.
